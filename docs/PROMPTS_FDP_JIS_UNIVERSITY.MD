Date : 7th July 2026

It was a pleasure and honour to conduct a session as part of the Faculty Development Program at JIS University Kolkata. The experience was truly enriching and inspiring. The session offered insights into AI agents, including key concepts such as agents, tools, and MCP, along with practical learning on building agents with the Agent Framework, integrating them with MCP tools, and understanding agent middleware and multi-agent architectures.
We discussed the importance of Agent Governance and the usage of the Microsoft Agent Governance Toolkit.
This also included a live demonstration with Jupyter Notebooks.

I truly appreciate the faculty members' engaging discussions, thoughtful questions, and enthusiasm throughout the session. I sincerely thank the organizers for their support and the participants for their active involvement and collaboration.

The session was heavily inspired by the Building your first agent in Python workshop by Pamela Fox [ Microsoft]
